Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Delvernauts ("the Game") collects and handles information when you use the in-game feedback form. The data controller is Sam / lemonscrap, contactable at sam@lemons.party.

What We Collect

When you submit feedback through the in-game feedback form, we may collect the following diagnostic data alongside your message:

  • Operating system and version
  • GPU model and driver version
  • CPU model
  • RAM amount
  • Screen resolution
  • Game version and build number
  • Current scene or level at the time of the report

This data is only collected when you submit a feedback report. We do not collect data in the background or at any other time. The feedback form includes an option to attach diagnostic data. You can check or uncheck this box before submitting.

We process this data based on your consent, given when you submit the feedback form. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us to request deletion of your submission.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or other contact information
  • Account identifiers (e.g., Steam ID, platform usernames)
  • IP addresses
  • Payment or financial information
  • Gameplay analytics or behavioral tracking data

How We Use Your Data

Diagnostic data is used solely for debugging and improving the Game. Specifically:

  • Identifying hardware or software configurations that cause bugs or crashes
  • Reproducing and fixing issues described in feedback reports
  • Improving compatibility across different systems

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than debugging and game improvement.

How We Store Your Data

Feedback submissions and any attached diagnostic data are sent to a private Discord channel accessible only to the development team. Submissions are automatically deleted after 30 days.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of diagnostic data collection by unchecking the checkbox on the feedback form before submitting.
  • Request early deletion of a specific feedback submission by contacting us at sam@lemons.party. Note that all submissions are automatically deleted after 30 days regardless.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we do not collect personal identifiers, most diagnostic data we receive is not linked to an identifiable individual. If you believe we hold data that identifies you, contact us and we will address your request.

Third Parties

We do not sell or share your data with any third parties. Feedback submissions are stored in a private Discord channel via a webhook. Discord hosts this data on our behalf, but you do not need a Discord account and do not interact with Discord at any point. All data requests, including deletion, are handled directly by us.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The feedback form does not require or request any personal identifiers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be noted by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the feedback form after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:

sam@lemons.party
